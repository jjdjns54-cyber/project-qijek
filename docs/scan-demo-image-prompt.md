Use authentic iPhone color science and ProRAW-like natural detail. Preserve realistic pores, fine facial hair, and consistent skin tone. Keep identity, clothing, background, exposure, white balance, and camera height identical across all six frames.

Avoid selfie perspective, wide-angle or fisheye distortion, portrait-mode blur, beauty filters, skin smoothing, glamour retouching, dramatic light, smile, head tilt, cropped hairline, cropped chin, facial landmarks, app UI, logos, and watermarks.
