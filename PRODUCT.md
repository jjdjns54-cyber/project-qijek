# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People considering appearance improvements who want a clearer, evidence-aware understanding of facial proportions before spending money or making a permanent decision.

## Product Purpose

DOODEE turns guided front and profile photos into an understandable analysis across 85+ facial measurements grouped into ten areas. The marketing site must establish trust, explain the analysis clearly and move users toward starting an analysis without promising a diagnosis or guaranteed outcome.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm and premium. The experience should feel editorial and product-led, with the restraint of Apple and the analytical clarity of FaceIQ Lab.

## Anti-references

Dark interfaces, heavy display type, crowded SaaS card grids, decorative glass everywhere, purple-heavy gradients, fabricated social proof, judgmental beauty scoring and generic AI-generated landing-page patterns.

## Design Principles

- Show the real analysis before explaining it.
- Create confidence through restraint, legibility and generous rhythm.
- Make every claim specific, verifiable and non-judgmental.
- Keep the primary journey focused on facial analysis.
- Treat privacy and user control as product features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The marketing site must meet WCAG 2.2 AA, support keyboard and touch interaction, preserve visible focus states, remain usable at 200% zoom and remove non-essential motion when reduced motion is requested.
