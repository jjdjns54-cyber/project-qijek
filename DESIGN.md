# DOODEE Design System

The canonical brand kit is [`brand-kit/README.md`](brand-kit/README.md). All AI-generated UI and assets must read it before making visual changes.

## Non-negotiables

- The interface must be light. Dark sections and purple hues are prohibited.
- The visual language must feel precise, calm, premium and product-led.
- Use ice blue, true white, near-black and cool blue-gray only.
- Use Manrope for all product and marketing typography.
- Layouts must use generous spacing, strong hierarchy and low visual density.
- Glass must be subtle, white and functional. It must not become decorative blur everywhere.
- Primary actions must use near-black with white text.
- Blue must communicate interaction, focus, measurement and analysis.
- Facial analysis must be non-judgmental. Never label a person beautiful, ugly, good or bad.
- Treatment previews must be described as illustrative, not guaranteed medical outcomes.
