# DOODEE AI Brand Context

Read this file before generating UI, copy, imagery, icons or treatment previews for DOODEE.

## Prompt anchor

DOODEE is a light-only facial analysis and treatment exploration platform. Create a precise, calm and premium interface with Apple-like restraint and FaceIQ-style analytical clarity. Use true white, ice blue, near-black and electric blue. Use Manrope, generous whitespace, low density, crisp alignment and subtle functional glass. Preserve natural human identity and avoid beauty-judgment language.

## Must preserve

- Brand name: DOODEE, always uppercase.
- Logo: double-D monoline mark plus widely tracked wordmark.
- Primary palette: white, ice blue, near-black and electric blue.
- Main CTA: near-black pill with white text.
- Visual tone: clear, quiet, analytical and human.
- Images: realistic texture and unchanged identity.

## Must avoid

- Dark themes or dark page sections.
- Purple, magenta, warm beige or gold.
- Decorative gradients, gradient text or excessive glow.
- Glass cards everywhere.
- Oversized radii, dense SaaS grids and floating decorative pills.
- Generic AI faces, waxy skin or unrelated facial changes.
- “Perfect”, “flaw”, “ugly”, “beauty score” or guaranteed outcomes.
- Fabricated users, analyses, research or clinical claims.

## Image-generation suffix

Natural high-resolution photography, realistic skin texture and pores, consistent white balance, soft daylight, no beauty filter, no waxy skin, no glam retouch, no AI sheen, no facial identity drift, no changes outside the requested anatomical region.

## Treatment-edit rule

Only edit the named anatomical region. Composite the result over the original with a soft feather mask. Preserve eyes, lips, nose, jaw, hair, skin tone, background, lighting and small details unless the selected treatment directly targets that region.
