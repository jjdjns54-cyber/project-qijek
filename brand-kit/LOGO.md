# DOODEE Logo

## Anatomy

- Mark: two nested monoline D forms.
- Wordmark: DOODEE in uppercase Manrope Semibold with `0.22em` tracking.
- Default lockup: mark left, wordmark right, 10px optical gap.

## Color

- On light backgrounds: near-black `#0B0E14`.
- On dark photography only: white `#FFFFFF`.
- Blue logo variants are not permitted.

## Clear space and size

- Keep clear space equal to half the mark height on all sides.
- Minimum mark height: 24px digital.
- Minimum complete lockup width: 112px digital.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or place the logo inside a decorative badge.
